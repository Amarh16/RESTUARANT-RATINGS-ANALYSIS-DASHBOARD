# RESTUARANT-RATINGS-ANALYSIS-DASHBOARD

Analyzed restaurant ratings, consumer demographics, cuisine preferences, and market demand using Power BI. Identified top-performing cuisines, high-rated restaurants, and demand-supply gaps to support strategic investment and business growth decisions.


![](https://github.com/Amarh16/RESTUARANT-RATINGS-ANALYSIS-DASHBOARD/blob/main/IMG_1666.jpg)


## Executive Summary
This analysis was conducted to identify the factors that influence restaurant success, understand consumer preferences, evaluate restaurant performance across cuisines, and uncover potential investment opportunities within the restaurant market. The dashboard combines restaurant ratings, customer demographics, cuisine preferences, demand versus supply analysis, and geographic distribution of restaurants.

The findings reveal that consumer demand is heavily concentrated around a small number of cuisine types, while restaurant supply is more fragmented across many categories. Mexican cuisine dominates customer preference, creating significant market opportunities. The analysis also shows that restaurant ratings vary across cuisines, suggesting that certain cuisine categories consistently deliver stronger customer experiences than others.

## Methodology Note:

The average rating of 1.11 reflects the structure of the public dataset used for this analysis and should not be interpreted as an industry benchmark for restaurant quality. Ratings within the dataset are concentrated within a relatively narrow range, making comparative analysis more meaningful than the absolute rating value itself. As a result, the analysis focuses on relative performance across restaurants and cuisines, consumer preferences, and demand-supply dynamics rather than using the average rating as a standalone measure of quality.

### Interpretation

The dataset contains 130 restaurants serving a market represented by 120 consumers across 21 cuisine categories. The average customer rating of 1.11 indicates moderate customer satisfaction. Since ratings are measured on a 0 to 2 scale, the overall performance suggests restaurants are generally meeting expectations but still have considerable room for improvement.

The relatively large variety of cuisine options indicates a competitive marketplace where restaurants must differentiate themselves through quality, service, and customer experience.

## 2. Restaurant Performance Analysis

### Highest Rated Restaurants
The Highest Rated Restaurants visual highlights establishments that consistently achieve the strongest customer ratings.

### Key Findings
- Several restaurants achieved the maximum observed rating of 2.00
- A small group of restaurants clearly outperforms the market average
- High-performing restaurants demonstrate that superior customer experiences are achievable within this market

### Insight
The performance gap between the highest-rated restaurants and the overall average rating suggests that customer satisfaction is not evenly distributed across all restaurants.

Some establishments have successfully aligned their offerings with customer expectations, while others may be struggling with service quality, menu offerings, pricing, or customer experience.

### Business Impact
Understanding the operational practices of top-performing restaurants can provide valuable benchmarks for underperforming establishments seeking improvement.

## 3. Cuisine Performance Analysis

### Key Findings

- Bakery

#### Lower-rated cuisines include:

### Business Impact
Investors and restaurant operators may benefit from focusing on cuisine categories with demonstrated customer satisfaction advantages.

High-performing cuisine categories may offer stronger opportunities for sustainable growth and customer loyalty.

## 4. Consumer Demographic Analysis

### Age Distribution

#### Key Findings

- Approximately 74% of consumers fall within the 21-30 age group
- Other age categories represent relatively small proportions of the customer base

#### Insight
The restaurant market is strongly influenced by young adults.

This demographic often prioritizes:

- Convenience
- Value for money
- Social dining experiences
- Trendy and diverse cuisine options

#### Business Impact
Marketing campaigns and menu development strategies should primarily target young adult consumers.

### Marital Status Analysis

#### Key Findings

- Approximately 92.5% of consumers are single
- Married consumers represent a small segment of the dataset

#### Insight
The market is dominated by independent consumers rather than family-oriented dining groups.

#### Business Impact
Restaurants may benefit from:

- Casual dining concepts
- Flexible seating arrangements
- Social dining experiences
- Affordable meal options

### Occupation Analysis

#### Key Findings

- Approximately 86.7% of consumers are employed
- Students represent the second-largest group
- Unemployed consumers account for a very small proportion

#### Insight
Most customers possess regular purchasing power and are likely making dining decisions based on convenience, quality, and value.

#### Business Impact
Restaurants can potentially increase revenue through loyalty programs, premium offerings, and targeted promotions for working professionals.

## 6. Demand vs Supply Gap Analysis
This section compares the number of restaurants available within each cuisine category against customer demand.

### Key Findings

#### Mexican Cuisine

Consumer Demand: 87

Restaurant Supply: 27

Although Mexican cuisine has the highest supply, customer demand still significantly exceeds available restaurant options.

#### Other Cuisine Categories

Most other cuisine categories show relatively balanced demand and supply levels, but none approach the demand intensity observed for Mexican cuisine.

#### Insight
The gap between consumer demand and restaurant supply identifies areas of potential market opportunity.

Mexican cuisine represents the clearest opportunity for expansion due to its substantial demand surplus.

#### Business Impact
Potential investors should prioritize:

- Mexican cuisine expansion
- Additional restaurant locations
- Franchise opportunities
- Enhanced menu specialization

This gap suggests strong potential for revenue growth and market penetration.

### Insight

## Overall Conclusions
The analysis identifies four major drivers of restaurant success:

#### 1. Cuisine Selection Matters
Customer satisfaction varies significantly across cuisine categories. Specialty cuisines generally achieve stronger ratings than mainstream alternatives.

#### 2. Young Adults Drive Market Demand
The majority of consumers belong to the 21-30 age segment, making them the most influential customer group.

#### 3. Mexican Cuisine Represents the Largest Opportunity
Consumer demand for Mexican cuisine substantially exceeds supply, indicating a strong opportunity for market expansion.

#### 4. Customer Experience Drives Competitive Advantage
Top-performing restaurants consistently achieve ratings well above the market average, demonstrating that operational excellence directly influences customer satisfaction.
